# AI transformation assessment

> A board-ready AI transformation plan and a working prototype before committing to a full build.

AgentPress identifies and validates automation opportunities around the client's business. An assessment takes about three weeks, depending on participant availability.

## Five deliverables

1. Validated automation opportunities ranked by value and feasibility.
2. Estimated ROI based on business value, build costs and running costs.
3. A board-ready AI transformation plan with an executive workshop.
4. A working prototype of a priority workflow, built with sample data.
5. An implementation roadmap with priorities and a phased rollout plan.

## Scope and participation

The assessment includes input from up to 500 employees, discussions with up to three department leads, review of three high-volume workflows and a two-to-three-hour executive workshop. Consultants validate the findings and business assumptions with the client team.

## Price and implementation

The assessment costs $15,000. 50% is credited to the first build. Production implementation is separately scoped; the assessment is not a commitment to a production build.

The client can use the findings with an internal team or hire AgentPress to implement. Estimated ROI is not a guarantee of results.
