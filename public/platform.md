# AgentPress platform

> Our secure platform connects SaaS and homegrown back-office systems.

## Deployment and security

The homepage describes SOC 2 Type II auditing and self-hosted or on-prem deployment, with AWS shown as one hosting option.

## Back-office connections

The homepage highlights Salesforce, Microsoft Dynamics 365, SAP, Oracle NetSuite, Outlook and Workday. Engineers configure the platform, integrations and workflow components around the client's systems and approval rules. The appropriate integrations and production requirements are confirmed during scoping.
