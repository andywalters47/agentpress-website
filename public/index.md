## How we work

Our AI agents interview employees about repetitive tasks, bottlenecks and exceptions. Our consultants validate the findings with the team before recommending what to automate.

We estimate each workflow's impact on revenue, costs, capacity and risk, then weigh that value against build and running costs. We validate assumptions with the team and prioritize the opportunities worth investing in.

We deliver prioritized workflows, estimated business value, a phased implementation plan and a sample-data prototype of one workflow before committing to production.

## Frequently asked questions

### What does AgentPress do?

We help mid-market companies find valuable automation opportunities, build the business case and implement the workflows. Our engineers combine existing platform capabilities with custom development and ongoing support.

### We already know our pain points. Why do the interviews?

Your existing work gives us a starting point. Employee interviews reveal the steps, exceptions and handoffs that determine whether an automation will work and create value.

### Who checks the AI's findings?

Our consultants and engineers validate the findings and assumptions with your team. They are responsible for the recommendations and delivery.

### How much involvement does our team need?

The assessment includes input from up to 500 employees, discussions with up to three department leads, review of three high-volume workflows and a two-to-three-hour executive workshop.

### How long does the assessment take?

About three weeks, depending on participant availability. We cover discovery, workflow analysis and presentation of the findings.

### What does the assessment include?

Employee interviews, prioritized opportunities, estimated ROI, a costed roadmap, a board-ready report and executive workshop, and one sample-data prototype. The assessment costs $15,000, with 50% credited to the first build. Production builds are scoped separately.

### Do you guarantee estimated ROI?

No. We validate the assumptions with your team, but actual results depend on implementation, adoption and how the resulting capacity is used.

### Can our own team implement the roadmap?

Yes. Your team can use the roadmap and workflow requirements to guide implementation, or have AgentPress build and support the workflows.
